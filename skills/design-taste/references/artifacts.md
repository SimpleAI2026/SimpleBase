# Visual Artifacts (posters, covers, static art)

For PNG/PDF pieces meant to be looked at rather than used.

## Two-step process
1. **Design philosophy (short manifesto, 3–5 paragraphs).** Name an aesthetic movement you invent for this piece; describe its approach to form, space, color, scale, rhythm, and texture. Emphasize that the result must look meticulously crafted — expert-level, labored over.
2. **Express it on a canvas.** Translate the philosophy into a composition where visual elements carry the meaning and text is minimal (a title, a label, a small caption).

## Craft rules
- Strong composition: clear focal point, deliberate negative space, alignment to an underlying grid or systematic pattern.
- Limited palette (2–4 colors + neutrals) with intent.
- Type as image: few words, sized with conviction; check every letter fits and nothing overlaps unintentionally or bleeds off the canvas.
- Repetition and systematic variation (patterns, marks, data-like structures) create sophistication.
- Originality: do not imitate a living artist's recognizable work; draw on movements and principles instead.
- Fonts: use locally available/licensed fonts; embed them in the PDF.

## Tools
Python (Pillow, reportlab, matplotlib, cairo), HTML/CSS → screenshot/print to PDF, or SVG. Render, inspect the image, and refine at least once ("make it more refined, not more stuff").

## Multi-page
Treat pages as a series: same system, varied compositions, like plates in a coffee-table book.
